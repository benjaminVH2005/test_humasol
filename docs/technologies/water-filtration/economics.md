# D. Economics

## What to take into account?

### Investment
- Components
- **Transport and import duties** (often underestimated!)
- Installation material and local labour

### During the lifetime
- Replacing parts
- Maintenance and travel costs of the technician
- Spare parts

## Key figures

| Key figure | What does it tell you? |
|---|---|
| Total investment | What the project costs at the start |
| Lifetime cost | Investment + replacements + maintenance |
| Cost per unit | e.g. per m³ of water or gas, to compare with alternatives |
