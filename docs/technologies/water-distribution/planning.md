# F. Planning: Water distribution

## How to start?

- [ ] Read the [past projects with Water distribution](index.md#past-projects-with-this-technology) and their lessons learned
- [ ] Read the country page and contact the local partner
- [ ] Create a project folder on the Drive, with the same structure as this site

## Planning within the academic year

| Period | Phase | Main tasks |
|---|---|---|
| Oct – Dec | Exploration | Map the partner's needs, read past projects |
| Jan – Mar | Design | Sizing, requesting quotes, having the design reviewed |
| Apr – Jun | Preparation | Fix the budget, order equipment |
| Jul – Aug | Implementation | Installation on site, training of the local technician |
| Sep | Handover | Write lessons learned, hand over to the next team |

## Planning over the years

| Year | What? |
|---|---|
| Year 1 | Design, installation and training |
| Year 2 | First evaluation, small adjustments |
| Year 3–5 | Yearly remote follow-up |
| After that | Full handover to the partner |
