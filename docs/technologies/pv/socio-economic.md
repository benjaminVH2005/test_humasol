# G. Socio-economic: PV (solar power)

## Economic
- Investment: components, **transport and import duties** (often underestimated), local labour
- Costs over the lifetime: replacing parts (especially batteries), maintenance
- Who pays for replacements later? (e.g. a reserve fund at the partner)

## Social
- *Who uses the system and how is the community involved?*
- *Training of local operators*
