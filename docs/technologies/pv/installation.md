# E. Practical installation

## Before you leave

- [ ] Load profile checked with the partner
- [ ] Quotes from local suppliers compared, with the **warranty of each component**
- [ ] Electrical diagram ready, with cable cross-sections, fuses and switches (see [4. Sizing](technology/sizing.md))
- [ ] Design checked by someone with electrical experience
- [ ] Local electrician or technician found and involved
- [ ] Maintenance manual and training material ready

## On site

1. **Mount the structure** and the panels, facing the equator, without shade.
2. **Connect the strings** as designed, with the string fuses, and measure the open-circuit voltage of each string **before** connecting it to the MPPT.
3. **Install the batteries** in a dry, ventilated, shaded room.
4. **Connect the DC side:** batteries, busbar, MPPTs and inverter, with all fuses in place.
5. **Connect the AC side** with the RCDs and circuit breakers, and **earth** all metal parts.
6. **Set up the monitoring** (Cerbo GX, VRM, 4G router) and check that data arrives.
7. **Test everything** before connecting the loads, then train the local operator.

## Safety

- Panels give voltage as soon as light falls on them: **cover them while connecting**, and use the DC disconnect switch.
- DC currents near the battery are very high: never short-circuit the battery poles, and remove rings and watches.
- Never work alone, and follow the safety analysis in your report.
