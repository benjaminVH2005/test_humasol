# E. Practical installation

## Before departure
- [ ] Load table checked with the partner
- [ ] Quotes from local suppliers compared, warranty per component asked
- [ ] Electrical diagram with cable sections, fuses and breakers ready
- [ ] Local electrician or technician found and involved
- [ ] Maintenance plan and training material ready

## What to watch out for
- Panels produce voltage as soon as light falls on them: **cover them while connecting**
- Batteries in a dry, ventilated room
- Use the right **cable sections** and protection (fuses, MCBs, RCDs, surge protection) and **earth** the system
- Test everything **before** connecting the loads
- Check for shade at different times of day
