# D. Economics

## What to take into account?

### Investment
- Panels, inverter, charge controllers, batteries, monitoring
- Mounting structure, cables, protection devices, earthing
- **Transport and import duties** (often underestimated)
- Installation material and local labour (e.g. a local electrician)

### During the lifetime
- **Battery replacement**: usually the biggest cost after the purchase
- Maintenance, spare parts, travel of the technician
- Internet subscription for remote monitoring (4G router)

### Savings to compare against
- Fuel for a diesel generator, or grid electricity bills (e.g. [Apida](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1c8-2AVDGSzAgYXFXR_jMqJk__LChXEvR/view), [Hart voor Senegal](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1N-7zGPKSlN5lW41Qz10BElMGGCMtTnW9/view))

## Tips from the projects

- When the budget is too small, **build in phases** ([Emergi](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1dvwuWhsSH8iRaCqRwZrG4B46ZKS-dGI-/view)) or start **without batteries** in a hybrid system ([Gitaru Marigu](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1D3iP_NHe1bnwG8g8spbsb6MS3Cgx5PEH/view)).
- Ask local suppliers for quotes early: prices and availability differ a lot per country.
