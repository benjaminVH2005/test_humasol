# D. Economics

## What does a PV system cost?

When you make the budget, include:

- **Components:** panels, charge controllers, batteries, inverter(s), monitoring (Cerbo GX, 4G router)
- **Installation material:** mounting structure, cables, fuses, switches, surge protection, earthing
- **Transport and import duties:** often underestimated
- **Local labour:** for example a local electrician

During the lifetime:

- **Battery replacement**: usually the largest cost after the purchase
- Maintenance, spare parts and travel of the technician
- The internet subscription for remote monitoring

## Compare with the alternative

A PV system is often cheaper over its lifetime than the alternative: diesel for a generator, or expensive grid electricity ([Apida](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1c8-2AVDGSzAgYXFXR_jMqJk__LChXEvR/view), [Hart voor Senegal](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1N-7zGPKSlN5lW41Qz10BElMGGCMtTnW9/view)). Calculate the **payback time**: investment ÷ yearly savings.

## When the budget is too small

- **Build in phases:** design the full system, build part of it now and let the partner or a later team add the rest ([Emergi](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1dvwuWhsSH8iRaCqRwZrG4B46ZKS-dGI-/view)).
- **Start without batteries** in a hybrid system and add them later ([Gitaru Marigu](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1D3iP_NHe1bnwG8g8spbsb6MS3Cgx5PEH/view)).
- **Compare panels and batteries:** extra panels are often cheaper than extra battery capacity ([Chaulmoogra](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1CjcOCPlSrWIydsPOc8KnPEB9xlv46_OT/view)).
- Ask local suppliers for quotes early: prices and availability differ a lot between countries.
