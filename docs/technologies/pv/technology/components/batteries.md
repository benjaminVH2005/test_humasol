# Batteries

## Info
Store energy for the night and for cloudy days.

## Relevant parameters

| Parameter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Capacity (Ah or kWh) | How much energy the battery holds |
| Depth of discharge (DoD) | How much you can use without damage |
| Number of cycles | Lifetime |
| Voltage (V) | 12/24/48 V system |

## Types + comparison

| Type | Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|---|
| Lead-acid | Cheap, often available locally | Shorter lifetime, sensitive to deep discharge |
| Lithium (LiFePO4) | Long lifetime, deep discharge possible | More expensive to buy |

## How to size it

1. Daily consumption × number of days of autonomy
2. Divide by the allowed depth of discharge
3. Divide by the system voltage to get the capacity in Ah
