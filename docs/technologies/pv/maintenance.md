# C. Maintenance & sustainability

A PV system can run for many years, but only if someone on site takes care of it and someone at Humasol keeps an eye on it. Plan this **before** you leave, together with the partner.

## What has to be done?

| How often | Task | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Regularly (e.g. weekly) | Remove dust, leaves and bird droppings from the panels; check that no new shade has appeared | Local operator |
| Monthly | Check that cables and connections are tight and undamaged; look at the battery state and the error messages | Local technician |
| Yearly | Full check: cabling, earthing, fuses and protection devices, mounting structure | Local technician, with Humasol Follow-up |
| End of battery life | Replace the batteries | Partner, with money set aside for it |

## Remote monitoring

Recent projects use a **Victron Cerbo GX** connected to the internet with a **4G router**. It sends all data to the **VRM portal**, where the Follow-up team can see the solar yield, the battery level and any alarms without being on site ([Chaulmoogra](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1CjcOCPlSrWIydsPOc8KnPEB9xlv46_OT/view), [Ayonnah](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1WEOuCyAQanaq12DLHQQtdQrMjhzrDj8N/view), [Emergi](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1dvwuWhsSH8iRaCqRwZrG4B46ZKS-dGI-/view)).

Remote monitoring only helps if somebody looks at it. Agree **who** at Humasol checks the data, **how often**, and **what happens** when something is wrong.

## The Emergi approach

The Emergi team made a complete plan to keep the system running five years after handover. It is a good model for other projects:

- a **preventive maintenance schedule**;
- **remote monitoring** with the Cerbo GX and VRM;
- **KPIs with green, yellow and red thresholds**, so it is clear when to act;
- a **field maintenance manual** for the local staff: system overview, safe working, routine maintenance, troubleshooting and how to expand the system;
- **local experts at three levels**: operator, maintenance and advanced.

## Make it last

- **Involve a local technician or electrician** in the installation, so they know the system ([Kiyodel](https://drive.google.com/file/d/124zqvYch7JHpEYUP_ul1__plQ9Rwe_94/view)).
- **Buy locally** where possible, so spare parts and warranty are available ([Kiyodel](https://drive.google.com/file/d/124zqvYch7JHpEYUP_ul1__plQ9Rwe_94/view), [Hope for Girls & Women](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Hf7LYZVS5rYBWHYoIerWkpYfLCCgrHsf/view)).
- Make sure the partner can pay for **battery replacement** later.
