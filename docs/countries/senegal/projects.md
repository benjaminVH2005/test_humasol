# Projects in Senegal

Projects Humasol has carried out here, based on the final reports on the student drive. Click a report for the full design, budget and lessons learned.

| 2022-23 | **KinkeliBa (Sokhna Mai)** | Nianing | PV | Off-grid PV for a women-led food-processing business (jams, cereals) and co-developing its expansion strategy. | [Open report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1RljsBJssF-sk2zSIMWka6DbazoWJropS/view) |
| 2022-23 | **Hart voor Senegal (Bay Dekk)** | M'bour | PV | Solar installation to cut the high electricity bills of a school with an electric water pump. | [Open report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1N-7zGPKSlN5lW41Qz10BElMGGCMtTnW9/view) |
