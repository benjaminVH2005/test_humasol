# Projects in Uganda

Projects Humasol has carried out here, based on the final reports on the student drive. Click a report for the full design, budget and lessons learned.

| Year | Project | Location | Technology | What was done? | Final report |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2024-25 | **Kiyodel** | Ntungamo District | PV | Off-grid PV installation with batteries for a skills centre for youth without access to regular education (lighting, laptops, projector, iron). | [Open report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/124zqvYch7JHpEYUP_ul1__plQ9Rwe_94/view) |
| 2024-25 | **Zombo Coffee** | Jukia micro-station | Solar drying | Solar dryer with a solar air collector and fan to shorten the drying time of coffee beans during the rainy season. | [Open report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/142XeA118LLbJ3JBHCcRTQ4icxnRecikK/view) |
| 2024-25 | **Sikiliza Leo** | Tororo region | Sanitation | Sustainable latrines for host families of orphans and vulnerable children, with composting of human waste instead of pit latrines. | [Open report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1lTo1Rg9ilD-hGS62V9wZyTA03ZuJTv6X/view) |
| 2023-24 | **Mbarara Water Purification** | Mbarara (MUST campus) | Water filtration, PV | Drinking water for a university campus: slow sand filtration, activated carbon and chlorination, powered by a PV system with batteries. | [Open report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1CwcN-04hN_bf4YehvvZrEdH0c8xXJ7xy/view) |
| 2022-23 | **Mbarara Irrigation** | Kabale, near Mbarara | Water distribution | Design of a sustainable, affordable irrigation system for a farm, using Uganda's relief and climate. | [Open report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1wSifi46Qzb2sEEgAF5HYzyxzR59l0QkU/view) |
| 2021-22 | **Ekisande** | Kisebere | PV, Water distribution | Solar power with batteries and grid backup for a primary school, and a solar pump for a 100–110 m deep borehole to provide drinking water. | [Open report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Uph_8Rsgz2uiQyqi_6xViWToYrFWDytj/view) |
